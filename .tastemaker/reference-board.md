## Quality bar
- Apple Music (web): light canvas, one red accent, track lists as the hero content
- Linear: type-led hierarchy, restraint at scale, nothing decorative in the first viewport
- Sonner docs: motion that confirms an action and stops

## Borrow
- Palette/material: Apple Music -> warm light canvas with a single saturated red-family primary
- Type/hierarchy: Linear -> big display headline, muted supporting line, one action
- Layout/composition: Apple Music -> numbered track rows with tabular numerals
- Motion/interaction: Sonner -> short ease-out entrances, press feedback, nothing looping except loading
- Asset language: Phosphor icons -> one rounded stroke family

## Avoid
- Spotify green as an accent (the product connects to Spotify; it is not Spotify)
- Dark slate + purple gradient canvases
- Emoji icons and feature-card walls

## Direction contract
- Thesis: the product's output is the visual
- First viewport: copy left, tilted example result card right
- System: warm canvas, tomato primary, teal accent, Urbanist display, CSS-only motion
- Risk: tomato used as large fills becomes loud; keep it to buttons, icons and one soft tint
